# Visual Design & Motion System: "Нить" (Thread Compliance OS)

## Aesthetic Thesis
- **Style:** Dark-mode Precision FinTech / Institutional Web3 Terminal (Linear meets Bloomberg meets Stripe).
- **Luxury Restraint:** Zero decorative slop, no gratuitous rainbow gradients, high optical contrast, razor-sharp typography.
- **Palette:**
  - Background Base: `#0B0E14` (Deep obsidian navy)
  - Surface Card: `#121824` / `#182030`
  - Border Subdued: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
  - Brand Accent: `#10B981` (Emerald Proof/Compliance Green) or `#38BDF8` (Cyan Precision)
  - Warning/Alert: `#F59E0B` (Regulatory Flag Amber), `#EF4444` (Critical Gap Red)
  - Text: Primary `#F8FAFC`, Secondary `#94A3B8`, Muted `#64748B`

## Motion Direction
- **Pacing:** Snappy, high-conviction micro-transitions (200–350ms cubic-bezier).
- **Hero Reveal:** Interactive graph topology nodes auto-connecting and turning green as compliance requirements are resolved by integrated vendor connectors.
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 4K / 1080p, optimized for BizDev calls, Loom pitches, and conference keynotes.
